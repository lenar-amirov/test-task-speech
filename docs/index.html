What happens to your recording

Cheap checks run on the phone, so a bad take is redone on the spot and never uploaded. The server checks again, since a phone can be tampered with, then listens for the words. Steps light up as your recording goes through.

On the phone

Record 16 kHz mono WAV
Phone checks length, silence, clipping, loudness, noise
Upload audio and the sentence shown

On the server · Supabase function qc-check

Check the sound again same rules, never trust the phone
Keep the recording private storage, for buyer audits and disputes
Listen for the words Whisper: which language, which words
Decide right language, text error within the limit
Log the decision Postgres qc.checks: the buyer report, the 10% batch rule and threshold tuning read from it

At scale the same function sits behind a queue. Borderline recordings, at most 5% of volume, go to a person instead of an automatic verdict. Accepted recordings are packed into buyer batches that must stay under the buyer's 10% rejection rule.

What this does not prove yet

  • The 97% and 2% come from defects planted in real recordings, so the right answer is known. Real buyer decisions are the next test.
  • FLEURS is read in quiet rooms. A car, a cheap microphone and free speech will be harder.
  • This server runs Whisper large-v3 (hosted), not Whisper small from the test, and English and Russian borrow the Spanish text limit. Neither is measured against the targets.
