DataTasks speech check

What the contributor sees

A driver or passenger opens a speech task in the app, reads one sentence and knows in a few seconds whether it is paid. Everything in this phone is live: the same check and server as the main page.

  • An answer in seconds, not tomorrowA bad take is re-recorded on the spot, while the person still has the sentence in front of them.
  • A reason they can act onNot "rejected", but what to fix: read the whole sentence, find a quieter spot, hold the phone further away.
  • A way to push backOne "check again", then "send it to a person". If the person accepts it, the task is paid as usual.

How the check works and how it was measured

Task types follow the DataTasks app: Voice, Dialogue and Comparison. The two voice tasks run the live check; dialogue and comparison are shown for context. Sentences in Spanish, Kazakh and Urdu are what the 95% / 2.7% were measured on; English and Russian are here to try it. The 75 ₸ per task is the price in the brief. Recordings are stored for this demo and the audio is deleted after 30 days.
