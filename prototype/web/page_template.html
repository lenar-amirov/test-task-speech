Speech QC before a recording is sent

Checks a read-speech recording the way a buyer would: enough speech, no long silence, no clipping, not too quiet or noisy, the right language, and the words of the prompt. The signal checks run here in your browser, as they would on a phone. Speech recognition (Whisper small) runs on the server; for the samples below its results are included.

How to check it in a minute

  1. Pick a sample. Under “Start here” below, click any button, for example “Heavy noise · Spanish”. These are real recordings the checks never saw while being tuned.
  2. Read the verdict. It appears next to the buttons: accept or re-record, the reason the contributor would see, and the seconds of speech we would pay for.
  3. See how often it is right. Scroll to “How we measured it”: share of bad recordings caught and good ones wrongly rejected, on recordings it never saw.

Your own file works too, but only the checks that run on the phone apply to it (length, silence, clipping, loudness, noise). The language and “said the right words” checks need the server, so they run for the samples only.

What this does not prove yet

  • Defects were planted on real recordings, so labels are known. Real buyer decisions (Common Voice reviews, then Buyer B's pilot) are the next test; Common Voice needs a signed-in download.
  • The fresh set has new sentences, but the defects come from the same generator. A defect type we did not think of is not measured.
  • FLEURS recordings are read in quiet rooms. Car noise, phone microphones and spontaneous speech will be harder.
  • Kazakh and Urdu recognition is weaker than Spanish, so their text check is looser.
