DataTasks speech check

Is this recording good enough to pay for?

Drivers and passengers read a sentence into their phone for a few tenge. Before we pay and ship it to a buyer, this check answers: accept, or re-record with a reason.

On 300 recordings it had never seen, this server caught 143 of 150 bad ones (95%) and wrongly rejected 3 of 150 good ones (2%).

What happens to your recording

Cheap checks run on the phone, so a bad take is redone on the spot and never uploaded. The server checks again, since a phone can be tampered with, then listens for the words.

At scale the same function sits behind a queue. Borderline recordings, at most 5% of volume, go to a person instead of an automatic verdict. Accepted recordings are packed into buyer batches that must stay under the buyer's 10% rejection rule.

What the check costs

Total≈ $3,500≈ $0.70

Listening to everything by hand would cost about $55,000 a month, or $11 per hour, so the check is about 15 times cheaper. It takes about 1.5% of the ~$45 a buyer pays per hour. List prices, November 2026.

What this does not prove yet

  • The 95% and 2% come from defects planted in real recordings, so the right answer is known. Real buyer decisions are the next test.
  • Subtle cheating is harder. Skipping a quarter of the sentence was caught only 23 times in 60 (38%); a person listening to borderline cases would raise that to 35 in 60. Reading a Kazakh sentence in Russian (40 of 40) and light clipping (60 of 60) were all caught. A word-by-word check is the next fix.
  • FLEURS is read in quiet rooms. A car, a cheap microphone and free speech will be harder. The free conversation task here runs on demo rules that have not been measured yet.
  • Fraud: the same file sent twice is caught. Replaying someone else's recording, text-to-speech and two accounts sharing one voice are not caught yet; they need speaker and synthetic-voice checks.
